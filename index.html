All your data is stored locally on your device only. Posy has no servers, no cloud storage, and no way to access your information. Your health data never leaves your phone.

Posy uses your logged cycle history to calculate predictions. The more cycles you track, the more accurate your predictions become. Predictions include period dates, fertile windows, and ovulation estimates up to 2 years into the future.

You can log your period flow (spotting, light, medium, heavy), mood (happy, calm, sad, anxious, irritable, energetic, tired, sensitive), and physical symptoms (headache, backache, breast tenderness, fatigue, nausea, cramps, and more).
